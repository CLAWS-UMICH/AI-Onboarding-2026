## What failed and why
- **Fine-tune v1 lost to the baseline on clean (0.922 vs 0.980).** Underfitting: head learning rate 1e-3 was too slow for MPNet's unit-length embeddings, and train accuracy was still rising at epoch 10. One-word pairs collapsed (secondary fan -> primary, scrubber B -> A). Fixed by tuning (head lr 1e-2, encoder lr 5e-5).
- **Laya zero-shot was near chance (0.21-0.23).** With 36 options it falls back to `unhandled` or the first option in the list (label/position bias), as its model card warns for base checkpoints. A general decision model doesn't replace training on in-domain data.
- **No label smoothing hurt** (val F1 0.910 -> 0.896); kept 0.1.
- **The frozen baseline barely benefits from noisy data** (attack +3 vs +12 for fine-tuning): it can't move "see oh two" closer to "CO2" in embedding space; fine-tuning can.
- **Augmentation can erase the label.** Substitutions like "two"->"too/to", "one"->"won", "scrubber a"->"scrubber eh" removed the only word separating primary/secondary or A/B. The critic caught and dropped them.
- **Test leakage through prompts.** The round 1 generator prompt included test_attack sentences as examples, so it copied them. `dedup.py` removed them; the blind `test_final_attack` is why the round's gain can still be trusted.
- **Agents don't always follow instructions** (the test-writer reused one RULES.md example verbatim). Every generated file was checked by script and by a critic.
- **Remaining weak spots (before round 1, from test_attack):** heavy speech-to-text spellings, mid-sentence corrections, and bare fragments like "pressure reading" that should be `unhandled`. Round 1 targeted these; some remain.
- **The 0.5 threshold trades accuracy for safety on noisy input** (cost 3 points before round 1, <1 point after). Kept because "say again" is safer than a wrong action on a suit HUD.
- **Slot extraction is rule-based** (drop command/filler words, keep the longest run): names containing those words lose them ("the lander" -> "lander").

## Decisions
- **Env:** uv (`pyproject.toml`, `uv.lock`).
- **Model:** `sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2`. Trained on short sentence pairs, which matches ~10-word commands. ModernBERT-embed dropped: its gains are long context and speed, irrelevant here.
- **Experiments run:** frozen MPNet + logistic regression (baseline), MPNet fine-tune, Laya zero-shot. SetFit (contrastive fine-tuning) skipped for time.
- **Labeling rules:** `data/RULES.md`. Core rule: label it if exactly one reading fits, else `unhandled`.
  - Unspecified readings use natural defaults ("how's my oxygen" -> primary O2 storage, "what's my CO2" -> suit CO2).
  - No natural default ("scrubber CO2", "coolant pressure") and two-command sentences are left out of training; `predict.py` handles them (returns both sides of the pair / splits on "and").
  - Noise around a full command -> real intent. Negated, past tense, system questions -> `unhandled`. "Finished X" -> `Complete_task`. Corrections -> the last instruction.
- **Name intents:** minimal rule-based slot extraction only (onboarding scope).
- **Data generation (no handwritten data):** separate subagents, each blind to the others:
  - Test-writer: `test_clean`, `test_attack`, `test_final` (150 each), later `test_final_attack` (150). Never sees training data, uses its own names.
  - 4 generators split by intent group; prompts force variety on form, speech style (radio/pilot, casual, stressed, speech-to-text errors), synonyms, length, contrast pairs.
  - Critic reviews labels and realism; `dedup.py` removes train rows near-identical (MPNet cosine >= 0.95) to a same-label test row.
- **Split:** stratified 85/15, seed 42 for every model; 3 training seeds (42/43/44) for spread.
- **Tuning:** choose by val macro-F1 only; clean/attack never used to choose. Top priority: no overfitting (train-val gap, val-loss trend, curves). Ties -> smaller gap, fewer epochs, lower learning rates, lower seed spread.
- **Threshold:** 0.5. Val accuracy is flat from 0.2 to 0.5; chose the safer end.
- **Improvement loop:** one round only. A sealed blind set (`test_final_attack`) is written before any data change and only its scores are read. `test_final` scored once at the end.
